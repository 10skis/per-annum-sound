Tape tuner. The ring goes; a wide band takes its place, like the frequency strip on an old car radio. One glyph and tick per station, a number every fifth, and the five groups marked underneath like MW and FM bands, with the current group lit. A fixed needle in the middle; drag, scroll or use the arrows to move the band under it.
