DISPLAY MODES

Three modes, dark to light, and one brightness slider for everything that carries information (lettering, lines, displays, art). Backgrounds stay put. Eclipse's tint is a slider with five notches; it snaps to each and blends between them. In the frames, tap the mode key in the header (LUN, ECL or SOL) for the panel the app would have.
