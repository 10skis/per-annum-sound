GALAXY MAP

The real app with a third map range, GALAXY. A barred spiral drawn as a halftone on the map's dot grid: NTS and the mixtapes in the bulge, one arm per group, each arm as bright as its group is big. Stations sit along their arm by longitude, so a city's stations cluster, and each arm's stations are joined like a constellation. The disc turns once every five minutes; the dots grow and shrink in place rather than sliding. Hover a star for its name and its city's other stations, click to play.
