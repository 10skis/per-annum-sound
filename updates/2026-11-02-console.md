# v0.16: Console look, nav map, waterfall, track info

**Date:** 2026-11-02
**Project:** Pi Radio

## What changed
- **Look.** Leaned into the "alien car dashboard" feel: panels have two chamfered corners, the console has registration brackets in its square corners, every screen has an inverse code tab (01 ART, 02 SIGNAL, 03 SPECTRUM, 04 NAV), big numerals have an unlit 88 behind them like a VFD, a tuning tape with a caret sits under the dial and the phone tuner, labels use Saira Condensed and the brand uses Michroma, and there's a faint dot-grid field and vignette behind everything. Panels light up in sequence on load; the map has a slow scan line. Both switch off with the system's reduced-motion setting.
- **Bottom deck.** The spectrum is now half width with a waterfall stacked under it (newest at the top), beside a new nav map. On phones they stack.
- **Map.** Dot-matrix land from Natural Earth's public-domain 1:110m outlines (`make_map.py` builds `static/worldmap.js`, 9 KB). A marker per city with station counts; WORLD and EUROPE ranges (buttons or M); tapping Europe's cluster zooms in; tapping a city lists its stations to play. The playing city pulses. The footer shows the current station's city and coordinates.
- **Track info.** Six stations publish what's playing to browsers and now show it: KEXP (artist and track, plus DJ notes), Dublab, Kiosk and Cashmere (Airtime), Refuge (radio.co), Noods (Radiocult). WQXR and WFMU block browsers, FIP's feed is gone, alHara has none.
- **Show reminders.** +CAL on each upcoming NTS show downloads a calendar event with a 5-minute alert.
- **Offline.** A service worker caches the page, station list, art and the last NTS data (network first); a NO SIGNAL note shows when the connection drops. Streams are never cached.
- **Help.** A small ? button in the corner (or the ? key) opens a panel of keys and gestures; Esc closes it.
- Stations now carry a place and map position: `places:` in stations.yaml, loaded by the new `catalog.py` for both the server and the build.

## Verified
- Desktop 1440px and phone 375px layouts, no overflow, header fits. Map zoom, city list, labels unclipped in Europe. Track info returned for all six stations. +CAL produced the right event. Help opens and closes. Server version loads with the map and KEXP info.
- Not verified: the service worker. The built-in test browser refuses to register any service worker; it needs checking on the iPhone after publishing.

## Why
- Robert asked to lean into the cyberpunk dashboard look, put a map beside a half-width stacked visualizer, build the remaining suggestions except idle dimming, add a ? shortcut panel, and run a UI/UX check with the new look in mind.

## Decisions made
- One wide face (Michroma) for the brand only, Saira Condensed for labels, VT323 kept for data, per the design survey's advice to stop at three faces.
- No red accent colour; the palette stays amber.
- Idle dimming left out, as Robert asked.
