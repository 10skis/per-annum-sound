# v0.40: Five more talk stations

**Date:** 2026-11-03
**Project:** Per Annum Sound

## What changed
- Talk Radio grows from two to seven: Monocle Radio (London), ABC Radio National (Sydney), CBC Radio One (Vancouver), RTÉ Radio 1 (Dublin) and LBC (London) join WESA and BBC World Service. WNYC skipped at Robert's call.
- New map cities: Vancouver (YVR) and Dublin (DUB).
- 64 stations; the dial now has 70 glyphs so each station keeps its own.
- Profiles checked against Wikipedia and Monocle's own pages (Monocle 24 launch, Radio National's 1985 rename); CBC, RTÉ and LBC dates are standard history.
- RNZ National was considered but its HTTPS stream doesn't answer, so it would only play on the Pi.

## Verified
- All five played in the browser (audio advancing, no error). 64 unique glyphs, rendering on the tape. Talk Radio band shows on the tape.
