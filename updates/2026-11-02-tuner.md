# v0.13: Phone tuner

**Date:** 2026-11-02
**Project:** Pi Radio

## What changed
- On phones the dial is gone. The art now runs full width with a tuner row under it: ◀, the station number (tap to play or stop), ▶. Swiping the art left or right also steps one station. Vertical drags still scroll the page.
- Each step plays straight away inside the tap or swipe, which iPhones require before they'll start audio.
- Desktop keeps the dial; the tuner is hidden there.
- Verified at 375px: arrows, swipe, vertical drag ignored, number tap toggles playback, no overflow. Verified at 1440px: dial shown, tuner hidden.

## Why
- Robert asked whether a dial suits a screen. Turning a dial with a finger is slow and imprecise, and it took half the top of the phone screen; the tuner keeps the flick-through browsing in a phone-native form.

## Decisions made
- Dial stays on desktop (mouse handles it fine) and on the physical device.
