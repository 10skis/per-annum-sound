# v0.81: Fluid fits its panel

**Date:** 2026-11-04
**Project:** Per Annum Sound

## What changed
- Fluid is sized from the panel's height as well as its width, so its tallest spike (1.37 R above the centre) and the outer cone ring (0.67 R below) always stay inside. Before, at the desktop panel's 81 px, spikes ran off the top.

## Why
- Robert saw Fluid cut off at the top and asked for a taller Level panel.

## Decisions made
- A taller panel was built (40 px more in the top row) and compared with fit-only in `mockups/level-height.html`. It shrank the whole console from 95% to 89% at 1440 x 900 for 12 px more panel on screen, and Robert couldn't see a difference, so it was dropped. Recorded in HANDOFF under Panel height.

## Verified
- 1440 x 900: console back at 95%, Fluid canvas 278 x 81, frames drawn by hand at full level stay between rows 26 and 166 of 170. 375 px: inside its canvas. `selfTest.layout()` clean at 1440.
