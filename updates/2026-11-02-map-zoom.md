# v0.23: Map click-to-zoom, softer dots

**Date:** 2026-11-02
**Project:** Per Annum Sound

## What changed
- **Click-to-zoom.** A click on the map away from any city eases in to 1.9× around that point; the next such click eases back out. Cities keep their size and spread apart when zoomed, the corner coordinates show the zoomed area (with ×1.9), and the world drift pauses while zoomed. Switching WORLD/EUROPE resets the zoom. Clicking a city still selects it.
- **City dots** a notch less bright (#e8c43a, hovered or playing #ffe56e) with a smaller glow.

## Verified
- Zoom in reached 1.9× and London's marker moved with it; no drift while zoomed; zoom out returned to 1×.
