# v0.34: Scroll to tune on the dial

**Date:** 2026-11-03
**Project:** Per Annum Sound

## What changed
- Scrolling over the desktop dial now steps through stations: down or right for the next, up or left for the previous. A mouse-wheel notch is one station; a trackpad takes about 40 px per station. The dial and tape preview as it goes and the station tunes once scrolling settles, like the tape. The page doesn't scroll while the pointer is over the dial.
- This was in the rotating-dial mockup but had not been carried into the app.

## Verified
- Trackpad 120 px down: 28 to 31. Two mouse notches up: 31 to 29.
