# v0.43: PAS sigil app icon

**Date:** 2026-11-03
**Project:** Per Annum Sound

## What changed
- The home-screen icon (180 and 512 px) is now a sigil like the no-artwork station art: dotted rings seeded by "Per Annum Sound" over the faint dot matrix, with PAS in the middle in VT323 with an amber glow. The two innermost rings are left out to make room for the letters. Opaque dark plate (#0e0c0a), so iOS masks it cleanly.
- It's drawn by the page itself (`dev/icon.js` calls the page's `sigilModel` and `drawSigilOn`), so it matches the in-app sigil exactly and needs no font download.
- `make_icons.py` (the old grey dial icon) is now a one-shot local server: it serves web/ on 8090 and writes the two PNGs the page posts back. Steps are in its docstring. The built-in browser pane blocks requests to a second local port, which is why page and receiver share one port.

## Verified
- Preview at 300, 120 and 60 px on a rounded mask; PAS readable at home-screen size. Files are 180x180 and 512x512 PNG, copied into web/static by the build.
