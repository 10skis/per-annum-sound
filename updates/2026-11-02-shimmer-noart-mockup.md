# v0.29: Stronger shimmer, no-art mockups

**Date:** 2026-11-02
**Project:** Per Annum Sound

## What changed
- **Art shimmer** is stronger and more frequent: the ripple is a wider, brighter ring with a softer echo ring behind it, travels further over 2.4 s, and the sweep band is broader. They now come every 3 to 8 seconds (was 5 to 14). Still only while playing, still off with reduced motion.
- **Mockup** `mockups/no-art-options.html`: five alternatives to the knocked-out name for stations without artwork, each shown on Christmas Vinyl HD, Give the Drummer Radio and Bangkok Community Radio: LED sign (initials on a matrix of unlit LEDs), location plate (map crop with marker and coordinates), sigil (record-like dotted rings seeded by the name), big glyph (the dial glyph in halftone), sound fingerprint (seeded ridge lines). Not built.
