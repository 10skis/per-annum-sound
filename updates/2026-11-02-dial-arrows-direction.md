# v0.28: Dial arrows match the tape

**Date:** 2026-11-02
**Project:** Per Annum Sound

## What changed
- The dial ring now lays stations out like the tape: upcoming ones on the right. ◀ turns the ring clockwise (the current glyph moves off to the right), ▶ anticlockwise, so the arrows move the ring the way they point. In v0.21 the ring had been flipped the other way, which made ◀ look like moving forward.
- Dragging the dial: the ring follows the hand. Turning clockwise brings up the station waiting on the left (the previous one); anticlockwise moves forward.

## Verified
- ◀ from station 29 to 28 moved the old glyph to +21° (right); ▶ moved it to -21° (left); a clockwise quarter-turn drag stepped back three stations.
